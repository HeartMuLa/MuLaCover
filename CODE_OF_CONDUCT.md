# Code of Conduct

MuLaCover is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community. Be considerate, discuss ideas rather than people, respect privacy,
and give constructive technical feedback.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained
disruption, publishing another person's private information, or sexualized and
otherwise inappropriate conduct.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
when behavior harms the community. To report conduct concerns privately,
contact the maintainers through the MuLa Labs organization and request a private
communication channel. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue or
Discord message.
